Our Story

Every celebration deserves a stage.

Vaidehi Events is a family-run event company from Korutla. We turn halls, homes and courtyards into places you will remember.

From a grand floral wedding arch to a simple marigold haldi corner, we plan, build and light every setup ourselves. Tell us your date and budget and we take care of the rest: flowers, drapes, stage furniture, lighting and balloons.
